Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

a person can wonder
unaware you know —
but sometimes
you should just wonder,
and other times
it’s better
to be unaware
though.

you know a person
can be unaware,
wondering —
then suddenly
aware
and full of wonder
at what is really
outside

and in front of them,
like the inner child
of an artist.

then they wonder
unaware,
wondering less
and less,
better
and better.
