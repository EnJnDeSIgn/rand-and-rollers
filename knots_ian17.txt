Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

angels are easy to let go of.
angels let go of shadows.
angels let go of sounds.
angels let go of sights.
angels let go of sensations.
angels let go of smells.
angels let go of tastes.

angels let go of shadows
that are feelings for people
and things already
let go of.

some people cling
to their heavy shadows —
the old memories,
the old harms,
the old stories
they refuse to release.

they hold their shadows close
even when the shadows
weigh on their senses:
their sounds grow harsh,
their sights grow dim,
their sensations dull,
their smells sour,
their tastes flatten,
their emotions twist,
their feelings warp.

and people who cling
to their shadows
do not like to let go
of angels —
especially when angels
have shadows
and they do not.
