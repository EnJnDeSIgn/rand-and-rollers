Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


i didn’t try to understand too much;
i don’t have attachment.
i don’t have attachment
because i don’t want to know.

we didn’t try to understand too much;
we don’t have attachment.
we don’t have attachment
because we don’t want to know.

they didn’t try to understand too much;
they don’t have attachment.
they don’t have attachment
because they don’t want to know.

if i can never know,
i don’t want to know,
and i won’t try
to understand.

a little girl comes along and says:

i don’t understand
what’s going on out there,
but it seems easier
not to understand it.
i don’t need to understand
to not care
what’s going on
in here.
