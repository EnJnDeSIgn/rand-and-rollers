Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

people seem to think
i should be ungrateful
because i don’t know
how to love too much —
that i would be more grateful
if i knew how to love too much
like you all.

but i am ungrateful
even while being asked
to be grateful
for not being allowed
to work
and live
without my parents.

for some reason
it is thought
i should be grateful
not to have to work
and still live
with my parents.

i am ungrateful
for being asked
to be grateful.
yet i find myself grateful
when i have something
to do —
whether people are grateful
or not
that i do it.
sometimes
it is enough
just to have
something
to do.
