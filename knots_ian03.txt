Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

i don’t care at all
about my fellow man —
not too much,
not enough.
they let go of me.
i let go of them.
i can respect
another person’s respect.

i don’t care at all
because i know how to love.
i am happy
because i know how to love.
they don’t care at all
because they know how to love.
they are happy
because they know how to love.

we are happy.
i am happy.
they are happy.
and all of us insist
we know how to love
because none of us
care at all.

we love
because we know
how not to care.
