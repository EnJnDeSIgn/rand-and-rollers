✦ The Wiccan Who Crossed the Road Only Once
(a Laing‑style knot)

The Wiccan crossed the road
only once,
because once was enough
to return him
to where he began.

And because he respected
the truth of beginnings,
time respected him back
by not moving.

And because time did not move,
nothing changed.
And because nothing changed,
everything began again.

And he will say,
as he always says,
that he crossed the road
only once,
because once
is the only number
that returns you
to 0.

And if you ask him
why he keeps coming back,
he will tell you
that he never left and
that just the truth.


✦ The Wiccan Who Crossed the Road Forever
(a self‑looping Laing knot)

The Wiccan crossed the road
only once,
because once was enough
to return him
to where he began.

And because he respected
the truth of beginnings,
time respected him back
by not moving.

And because time did not move,
nothing changed.
And because nothing changed,
everything began again.

And he will say,
as he always says,
that he crossed the road
only once,
because once
is the only number
that returns you
to 0.

And if you ask him
why he keeps coming back,
he will tell you
that he never left
and that’s just the truth —
the kind of truth
that brings him
right back
to the moment
when the Wiccan crossed the road
only once…
