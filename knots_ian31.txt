Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

if we don’t tell them
what we think is the matter
with them,
we can pretend
there is something the matter
with them
for not knowing
what’s the matter
with them.

Ian scratches his head and says,
let me get this straight:

you’re not going to tell me
what you think is the matter
with me —
and now that’s
what’s the matter
with me,
that i don’t know
what is the matter
with me?

and you think
i’m the crazy one.
