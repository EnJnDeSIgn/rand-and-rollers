Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

if you want it,
you don’t really
need it.

if they want you
to want it,
you probably
don’t want it.

if you need it,
you want it.
if you need it,
you won’t
need it.

you never need
what you want.
you wanted
to need it
even though
you never needed it
once you were done
wanting it.
