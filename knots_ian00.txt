Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

thoughts that refuse to let go
become the ones I call evil,
especially when the thought
is the one that makes
someone hard to let go of.

he lets go of him
she lets go of her
he lets go of her
she lets go of him

they try to let go of them
we try to let go of her
he tries to let go of them
we try to let go of him
she tries to let go of they

we let go of you
you let go of us
they let go of me
and I keep trying
to let go of the thought
that none of us
can let go.
