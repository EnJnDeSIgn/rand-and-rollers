Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

she doesn’t like him anymore.
now he likes her.
now she likes him.
now he doesn’t like her
anymore.

anymore, she doesn’t want
to be like him.
anymore, he doesn’t want
to be like her.
she is never like him.
he is never like her.

i like you
because you are not like me.
we have more to offer
each other
because we are not the same —
but different,
making us the same
in being
different.
