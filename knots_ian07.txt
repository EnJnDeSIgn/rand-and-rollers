Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


the one i really love
will be the easiest to let go of.
the one that is easiest to let go of
is the one i love.

because if i truly love,
i will not care at all about them.
because if i can never understand,
or know,
i will love too much.

it is the ones we care most about
we most need
to let go of.

i don’t want to be evil.
my father didn’t raise me
that way.
even if he respected,
he didn’t know
he could love too much.

we need the most to let go of
the ones
we care most about.

we care,
so we don’t understand
what’s going on out there.
but it seems easier
not to understand,
and we don’t need to understand
to not care
what’s going on in here —
with the ones
we care most about.
