Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

i’m not one to love too much.
i don’t care at all
about my fellow man —
not too much.

but you all
seem to love too much.

so i can only
try to understand,
and not want to know.
i can never know.

but you all
seem to not care at all
about your fellow man —
not too much.

so you can only
try to understand,
and not want to know.
you can never know.

and none of us knows
whether we love too much
because we don’t care,
or don’t care at all
because we love too much.
