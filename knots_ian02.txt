Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

i respect the ones
i don’t care about at all.
i raised my friends that way,
or at least
i thought i did.
a friend respects
that i don’t care at all.

of course i must be
worthy of respect.
i can respect
a friend’s respect.
but i hope, at least,
that my friend will respect me —
if only for leaving them
to respect the people
who respect me,
or don’t.

and so i respect them
for respecting me
for not caring,
and they respect me
for caring enough
to say i don’t.
