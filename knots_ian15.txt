Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

they try to run my cycle
and don’t know
they can’t.
they don’t see
they aren’t running
my cycle —
they are running
their own.

they run their own cycle
the way they would
try to run my cycle,
and don’t know
they don’t see
they aren’t running
my cycle
at all.

they run their own cycle
the way they would
never run
their own cycle
if they knew
it was theirs.
