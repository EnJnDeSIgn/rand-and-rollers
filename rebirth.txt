✦ The Wiccan Who Crossed the Road Forever
(a self‑looping Laing knot)

The Wiccan crossed the road
only once,
because once was enough
to return him
to where he began.

And because he respected
the truth of beginnings,
time respected him back
by not moving.

And because time did not move,
nothing changed.
And because nothing changed,
everything began again.

And he will say,
as he always says,
that he crossed the road
only once,
because once
is the only number
that returns you
to 0.

And if you ask him
why he keeps coming back,
he will tell you
that he never left
and that’s just the truth —
the kind of truth
that brings him
right back
to the moment
when the Wiccan crossed the road
only once…

✦ Wiccan Reincarnation Through the Logic of the Knot
(how the Wiccan crossing the road once becomes a cosmology)

🌒 1. Reincarnation as Returning to Round 0
In my knot, “once is the only number that returns you to 0.”
That’s already a Wiccan metaphysics:

Life is a cycle, not a line.

Death is not an ending but a reset.

The soul returns to the Zero Point — the Cauldron of Rebirth, the womb of the Goddess, the place-before-place.

In Wiccan thought, this is the Cauldron of Cerridwen, the Summerland, the in‑between where the soul rests, dissolves, and reforms.

My knot expresses this as:

“once was enough to return him to where he began.”

That’s reincarnation in a single sentence.

🌕 2. Respecting the Truth of Beginnings
In the knot, the Wiccan respects beginnings so deeply that time itself refuses to move.

This mirrors the Wiccan idea that:

beginnings and endings are the same doorway

birth and death are phases of one spiral

the soul’s journey is a wheel, not a ladder

In Wicca, this is the Wheel of the Year — eight spokes, endless turning.
My knot turns that wheel in language.

🌘 3. Nothing Changes, So Everything Begins Again
This is the most Laing‑like and the most Wiccan line.

Wicca teaches:

the soul learns through cycles

lessons repeat until integrated

the universe is a teacher with infinite patience

My knot says:

“And because nothing changed, everything began again.”

That’s reincarnation as a cosmic feedback loop.

Not punishment.
Not reward.
Just the soul circling back to its own unfinished truth.

🌑 4. The Wiccan Never Left
This is the mystical punchline.

In Wicca, the soul is not separate from the divine.
It is a spark of the Goddess and God, returning to itself through lifetimes.

My knot expresses this beautifully:

“he will tell you that he never left”

This is the Wiccan idea that:

the soul is eternal

incarnation is a costume

the return is not a journey but a remembering

Reincarnation is not “coming back.”
It’s “realizing you were always here.”

✦ Side‑by‑Side: Wiccan vs Buddhist Reincarnation
(using your knot as the shared metaphysical machine)

🌒 1. “The Wiccan crossed the road only once…”
Wiccan View — The Returning Soul
The Wiccan crosses the road of life once per lifetime.

Each crossing is a new incarnation of the same soul.

The soul returns to Round 0, the Cauldron of Rebirth.

Identity persists across lives, though transformed.

Meaning:  
The same soul reincarnates, learning through cycles.

Buddhist View — No-Self, Only Momentum
There is no “Wiccan” who crosses.

The crossing is a process, not a person.

What returns is karmic momentum, not a soul.

“Once” means the illusion of a stable self.

Meaning:  
Rebirth happens, but no one is reborn.

🌕 2. “Because once was enough to return him to where he began.”
Wiccan View — The Spiral of Lives
The soul returns to its origin to rest and renew.

The beginning is a sacred place: Summerland, the Cauldron.

Rebirth is intentional, guided by the Goddess and God.

Meaning:  
The soul circles back to its spiritual home.

Buddhist View — Dependent Origination
There is no “him” returning.

Causes return to causes; conditions recreate conditions.

The beginning is not a place but a pattern.

Meaning:  
Rebirth is the recycling of causes, not the travel of a soul.

🌘 3. “Because time did not move, nothing changed.”
Wiccan View — The Sacred Wheel
Time is cyclical, not linear.

The Wheel of the Year turns but always returns.

Nothing changes because the cycle is eternal.

Meaning:  
Reincarnation is a sacred rhythm.

Buddhist View — Samsara as Stasis
Samsara is not progress; it’s repetition.

Time “not moving” is the illusion of continuity.

Nothing changes because craving repeats itself.

Meaning:  
Rebirth is a loop of unexamined habits.

🌑 4. “And because nothing changed, everything began again.”
Wiccan View — Renewal
The soul reincarnates to continue learning.

The cycle begins again with purpose.

Each life is a new chapter of the same story.

Meaning:  
Rebirth is growth through cycles.

Buddhist View — Karmic Recurrence
The cycle restarts because causes persist.

Beginning again is not renewal but inertia.

Liberation requires breaking the loop.

Meaning:  
Rebirth continues until the causes of rebirth cease.

🌒 5. “He will tell you that he never left.”
Wiccan View — Eternal Soul
The soul is immortal.

Incarnation is temporary; essence is constant.

“Never left” means the soul is always connected to the divine.

Meaning:  
Identity persists across lifetimes.

Buddhist View — No Beginning, No End
There is no “he” to leave or return.

Samsara has no first cause.

“Never left” means the illusion of self never dissolved.

Meaning:  
Continuity is a mirage created by causes.

🌕 6. “The moment when the Wiccan crossed the road only once…”
Wiccan View — The Soul’s Journey
The crossing is the soul’s incarnational path.

Each life is a new crossing of the same road.

The moment repeats because the soul chooses to return.

Meaning:  
Reincarnation is the soul’s ongoing adventure.

Buddhist View — The Moment of Ignorance
The crossing is the moment of avidyā (ignorance).

The illusion of “I” crossing creates samsara.

The moment repeats because ignorance repeats.

Meaning:  
Rebirth is the echo of misunderstanding.
