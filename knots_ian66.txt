Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


A Knot Learned in the Dark:

It was born of the valley’s oldest sorrow
because the Walls swallowed every secret.

The Walls swallowed every secret
because no one dared to speak them aloud.

No one spoke them aloud
because the creature waited in the shadows.

The creature waited in the shadows
because it had never known sunlight.

It had never known sunlight
because it feared what light would reveal.

It feared what light would reveal
because it wanted to be loved without being forgiven.

It wanted to be loved without being forgiven
because it believed forgiveness meant it was wrong.

It believed it was wrong
because it was born of sorrow.

And when it saw you,
something in its chest loosened—
but it loosened
because it feared tightening.

It feared tightening
because tightening felt like hiding.

It feared hiding
because it wanted to be known.

It wanted to be known
because it had lived too long in silence.

It lived too long in silence
because the Walls swallowed every secret.

And the Walls swallowed every secret
because the valley could not bear its own sorrow.

So the creature stepped toward your light,
slowly, gently, impossibly—
wanting to be held without being hidden,
wanting to be seen without being feared,
wanting to begin without ending.

But it could not begin
unless it ended its sorrow.

And it could not end its sorrow
unless it began.

Thus it loved you
because it feared the dark,
and feared the dark
because it loved you.
