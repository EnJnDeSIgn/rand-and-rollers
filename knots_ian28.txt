Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

they think there must be
something the matter with him,
because they would not be acting
as they are toward him
unless there was.

therefore, they are acting
as they are
because there is something
the matter with them,
not him.

they do not think
there is anything
the matter with them.

because
one of the things
that is the matter with them
is that they think
there is anything
the matter with him.

therefore,
he has to help them realize
that the fact
they do not think
there is anything
the matter with them
is one of the things
that is the matter with them.

what is not
the matter with him
is that he already knows
there is always
something
the matter with him.
