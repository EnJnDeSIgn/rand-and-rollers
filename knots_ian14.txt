Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

they are playing games with him.
they are playing
at not playing games
with him.

he must play their game
whether he sees
or does not see
the games
they play.

they are never
not playing games
with him.
but if they show him
that they are,
they break the rules —
and he can call
shenanigans.

so they must keep playing
the game
of not playing
the game,
because the only rule
of their game
is that he must never
see
the game.
