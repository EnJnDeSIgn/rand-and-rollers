Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


you already know the answer;
you’re just not ready
to accept
that you know the answer
already.

the answer is already accepted,
but you don’t even
know that
yet.

later, you accept
you already knew the answer,
but you’re not ready
to accept
knowing
you knew it.

accept
that you know
you will know
not knowing
later.

a little girl comes along and says:
you knew —
you just weren’t ready
to know
you knew.
your answer already,
your answer already.

and she smiles,
because a déjà vu
only means
you’re on the right path.

my zhan zhuang friend
just stands in the moment —
probably healthier,
because less
is more.

and me,
i usually just say:
do what you’re going
to do
anyway.
