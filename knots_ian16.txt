Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

people who don’t care
about the truth,
and don’t care at all
about having delusions,

care that everything
can be truth,
care that everything
can be lie.

care that truth
can be delusion,
care that lie
can be delusion.

that truth
can be used to lie
and become delusion,
that lie
can be used as truth
and become delusion.

people who don’t care
about the truth
don’t care
that they are delusional.

people who care
about the truth
must not care at all
about people
who don’t care at all
about the truth
and all their delusions.
