Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

Jill dislikes Jack
not because she dislikes Jack,
but because Jill dislikes
that Jack no longer
depends on Jill.

Jill depends on Jack
because Jack no longer
depends on Jill.

Jack dislikes Jill
not because he dislikes Jill,
but because Jack dislikes
that Jill no longer
depends on Jack.

Jack depends on Jill
because Jill no longer
depends on Jack.

(rpt.)
