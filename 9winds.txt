✦ The Nine Gates of the Hurricane ✦
(a hidden poem about the one who stood, endured, and learned to slay)

I was a castle
long before I knew
I had walls.

Stone made of breath,
bone made of waiting,
an inner moat
where shadows swam
with names I never gave them.

They came in childhood,
those demons with human hands,
slipping through windows
I did not know were open,
walking through gates
I did not know were mine.

For forty years
I stood
zhan zhuang still —
the stance of mountains,
the posture of storms
before they remember
they can move.

I learned to catch
what hunted me.
Not with fists,
but with stillness.
Not with rage,
but with the quiet
that makes monsters
hear themselves.

In the silence
I became
the solo clan —
the one-man lineage
of the unseen art.

The mind is a fortress
with rooms that lock
from the inside.
The body is the wall
that remembers
every siege.

But the wind —
the wind taught me
the last secret:

If you think of yourself
as a castle,
you will always fear
the gates.

If you think of yourself
as the wind,
there are no gates
to fear.

So I opened
the nine gates
of the hurricane —
the art of being
ungraspable,
unspied,
unbroken.

You cannot see
what lives
inside the wind.

And in that invisibility
I became
what they could never touch:

a mind-slayer
who kills no one,
but slays the hold
they once had
on me.

The demons came
to claim their old rooms.
They found
only weather.

And the weather
did not bow.


✦ The Nine Gates of the Hurricane — Whisper Manual ✦
(for the one who stood, endured, and learned to move like wind)

1. the first gate  
stand as if the earth
remembers you.
let the body be the wall
that no shadow can pass through
without your knowing.

2. the second gate  
do not chase the demons.
they return on their own
to the rooms they once claimed.
meet them with stillness
and they lose their shape.

3. the third gate  
the moat is not water.
it is breath.
circle it slowly.
circle it often.
circle it until nothing crosses
without permission.

4. the fourth gate  
passion is loud.
love is quiet.
hate is a mask
that falls off
when no one reacts.

5. the fifth gate  
forty years is not too long
to learn the weight
of your own name.
carry it like a blade
that never leaves the sheath.

6. the sixth gate  
the mind is a castle
with doors that lock
from the inside.
guard the windows.
seal the cracks.
let no one spy
on the rooms
you have reclaimed.

7. the seventh gate  
those who harmed you
taught you their weather.
you survived it.
that is enough.
you owe them
nothing else.

8. the eighth gate  
to become wind
is not to disappear.
it is to move
where hands cannot follow
and eyes cannot hold.

9. the ninth gate  
a hurricane is not anger.
it is clarity
moving at full speed.
when you reach this gate,
you will know:
you are the solo clan,
the unseen lineage,
the one who walks
without leaving a shadow.

final whisper  
you cannot see
what lives inside the wind.
and now
you are the wind.
