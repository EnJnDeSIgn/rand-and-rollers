Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

if you want to love someone,
first you must learn
to love yourself.

if you hurt anyone else,
you will only
hurt yourself.

if you hurt yourself,
you haven’t learned
to love yourself.

if you hurt yourself
to not hurt
someone else,
you may be hurting
someone else
or you may be hurting
yourself.

if we learn something,
the hurt may be less
than how much
we learned
to love
ourselves.
