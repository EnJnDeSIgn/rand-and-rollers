Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


a voice speaks the statement
because the finger
had a point.

the ear hears the statement
but does not know
how to point.

the brain sends all the fingers
to poke the point,
to feel the points
that have not yet
become statements.

the tongue wiggles and says:
is interesting
is not interesting
tastes good
does not taste good

and the voice waits
for the finger to point again,
so the ear can hear again,
so the brain can poke again,
so the tongue can judge again,
until the statement
finally points
back at itself.
