Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

i don’t know too much,
so i try not to know.
i try not to know,
so i don’t know too much.

i know too much
because i try not to know.
i don’t know too much
because i know too much.
i know too much
because i don’t know too much.
i try not to know
so i will know too much.

it is not usually good
to know too much,
because knowing too much
doesn’t help us
know too much.

we know
by not knowing.
i know
that i don’t know.
and some days
that is enough.
