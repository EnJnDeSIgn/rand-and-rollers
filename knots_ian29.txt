Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris

they tried to run my cycle,
but ran their own cycle
the way they would never
run their own cycle —
so they blamed me
for how they ran
their cycle.

they try to run
their own cycle
and don’t know
they can’t,
and don’t see
they didn’t run
my cycle
first.

and they have to know,
then see,
that we all reap
what we sowed
ourselves —
when they didn’t want,
as i do,
to not run
other people’s
cycle.
