Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


the problem with people
who are hard to let go of
is that they make
someone else
hard to let go of.

they want to get away
with things
they should not
get away with.

they should not
get away with
the things
they get away with
when they make
someone else
hard to let go of.

people don’t see
the things
they get away with
when they make
someone else
hard to let go of.

it is thought
that they are easy,
and someone else
is hard to let go of.

and the things
they get away with
are the very things
people don’t see
them get away with.
