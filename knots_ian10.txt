Ian's new knots: by Ian J Norris


Jesus was having fun
playing the game,
but we are doing it wrong
if we don’t know
there is always something
the matter with us,
even when it is hard work.

there must be something
the matter with him.
he wouldn’t be acting
like they were
unless there was something
the matter with them.
and the one thing
the matter with us
is that we never thought
anything was the matter
with me.
